### REGISTER NUMBER: 212224240015

## Aim:

To write the prompts for the following prompt types and evaluate them using an AI-powered customer support chatbot:

1. Straightforward Prompts
2. Tabular Format Prompting
3. Preceding Question Prompting
4. Missing Word Prompting

## Explanation:

### Use Case: AI-Powered Customer Support Chatbot

An AI-powered customer support chatbot is designed to assist customers with **product troubleshooting, order tracking, and general inquiries**. Different prompt engineering techniques are used to generate suitable responses for customer queries.

## Procedure:

### 1. Straightforward Prompts:

**Prompt:**

> "My headphones are not turning on. Provide troubleshooting steps in a simple and polite manner."

**ChatGPT:**

> I'm sorry you're having trouble with your headphones. Please make sure they are charged, connect them to a power source, and press and hold the power button for a few seconds. You can also try a different charging cable. If the problem continues, please contact customer support.

**Gemini:**

> I can help you troubleshoot your headphones. First, ensure they have enough charge. Connect them to the charger and try holding the power button for a few seconds. If they still don't turn on, try another charging cable or adapter. If the issue persists, contact technical support.

**Evaluation:**

Both ChatGPT and Gemini generated relevant and user-friendly responses. ChatGPT provided a slightly more direct troubleshooting response, while Gemini presented the instructions in a conversational manner.

---

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting:

**Prompt:**

> "Provide the possible causes and solutions for a laptop that is not charging in a table with the columns Issue, Possible Cause, Troubleshooting Step, and Recommended Action."

**ChatGPT:**

| Issue               | Possible Cause        | Troubleshooting Step        | Recommended Action             |
| ------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Laptop not charging | Faulty charger        | Check the charger and cable | Try another compatible charger |
| Laptop not charging | Damaged charging port | Inspect the charging port   | Contact technical support      |
| Laptop not charging | Battery problem       | Check the battery status    | Request battery inspection     |
| Laptop not charging | Power outlet issue    | Try another outlet          | Use a working outlet           |

**Gemini:**

| Issue               | Possible Cause     | Troubleshooting Step        | Recommended Action             |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Laptop not charging | Faulty adapter     | Check the adapter and cable | Try another compatible adapter |
| Laptop not charging | Battery issue      | Check the battery status    | Restart and check battery      |
| Laptop not charging | Damaged port       | Inspect the charging port   | Contact service support        |
| Laptop not charging | Power source issue | Try another power outlet    | Use a working outlet           |

**Evaluation:**

Both AI tools followed the requested tabular format and presented the troubleshooting information clearly. The table makes it easier to identify possible causes and corresponding solutions.

---

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting:

**Prompt:**

> "What information should a customer support chatbot collect before checking the status of a delayed order? Now answer this query: My order hasn't arrived yet. Can you help me?"

**ChatGPT:**

> I'm sorry that your order hasn't arrived yet. I can help you check its status. Please provide your order number so I can help identify the order and check its current status. If available, you can also provide the tracking number.

**Gemini:**

> I'm sorry for the delay. I'd be happy to help you track your order. Please provide your order number or tracking ID so that the shipment status and estimated delivery date can be checked.

**Evaluation:**

Both AI tools understood the preceding question and generated focused responses. The preceding question provided additional context and guided the AI to request the required order information.

---

### 4. Missing Word Prompting:

**Prompt:**

> "Complete the missing word based on the customer-support context:
> Customer: My order hasn't arrived yet.
> The chatbot should ask the customer for their ________ before checking the order status."

**ChatGPT:**

> Order number.

**Gemini:**

> Order number.

**Evaluation:**

Both ChatGPT and Gemini correctly identified the missing information as **order number**. Missing word prompting is useful for obtaining a specific word or piece of information from a given context.

---

## Conclusion:

The four prompt engineering techniques were applied successfully to the AI-powered customer support chatbot scenario. Each prompting technique produced different types of responses depending on the structure of the prompt. Straightforward prompting provided direct responses, tabular prompting organized information clearly, preceding question prompting provided additional context, and missing word prompting generated specific information.

## Result:

Thus, the **Straightforward, Tabular Format, Preceding Question, and Missing Word prompts** were executed successfully and the generated AI responses were evaluated.
